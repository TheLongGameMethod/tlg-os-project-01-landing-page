THE PROBLEM

Most people practice without a system.

They watch tips, try random fixes, and hope something works. TLG OS gives them a structure: assess the truth, diagnose the weak point, correct one thing first, execute the plan, track the work, and prove the progress.

THE METHOD

Educate → Assess → Diagnose → Correct → Execute → Track → Prove → Advance

Assess

Find the truth before prescribing the fix.

Diagnose

Identify the highest-priority bottleneck.

Correct

Build a simple plan around the fix-first priority.

Prove

Track evidence that shows the work is actually improving results.
